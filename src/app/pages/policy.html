SUMMARY

How we collect or obtain information about you:

Information we collect: name, contact details, IP address, information from cookies, information about your computer or device, company name or business name (if applicable), VAT number (if applicable).

How we secure your information: using appropriate technical and organizational measures such as storing your information on secure servers, encrypting transfers of data using SSL technology, only granting access where necessary.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT WHEN YOU VISIT OUR WEBSITE

Web Server Log Information

Our website server automatically logs the IP address you use to access our website as well as other information about your visit such as the pages accessed, information requested, the date and time of the request, your browser version and operating system.

Our server is located within the European Economic Area.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

A cookie is a simple text file that is stored on your computer or mobile device by a website's server. Each cookie is unique to your web browser and allows a website to remember things like your preferences or what's in your shopping basket.

Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can alter the settings of your browser to erase cookies or prevent automatic acceptance if you prefer.

AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING AND PROFILING

We use profiling on our website but we do not use automated decision-making.

Our web analytics service uses and collects information such as your IP address and a randomly generated unique identifier using cookies. We will only process information from cookies if you have consented to us setting cookies on your computer.

We use web beacons in our marketing emails to analyse who opens our emails and what actions they take. We will only process information from web beacons if you have consented to their use.

HOW LONG WE RETAIN YOUR INFORMATION
